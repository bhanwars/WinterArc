PRIVACY POLICY · LAST UPDATED OCTOBER 1, 2026

Your goals stay yours.

Winter Arc is a local-first goal tracker. This policy explains what the app stores and what happens when you choose to use its optional AI day planner.

Goal and check-in data

Your goals, categories, schedule, and daily check-ins are saved on your device in the app's local browser storage. Winter Arc does not send this tracker data to its developer and does not require an account.

Optional AI day planner

The Android app can create a simple starter plan on-device without an AI connection. In the self-hosted web version, if you enable the AI planner, the app sends goal titles, completion status, streaks, your focus note, and energy choice to the Ollama service configured on the machine running Winter Arc. That machine may be your computer or a cloud development environment you chose to use. The app developer does not operate that service or receive the request.

Fonts and network access

The interface may request fonts from Google Fonts. That service can receive standard connection information such as your IP address and browser request metadata. Core goal tracking remains available without an AI connection.

Children

Winter Arc is a general productivity tool and is not designed specifically for children under 13.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through the Winter Arc GitHub issue tracker.
